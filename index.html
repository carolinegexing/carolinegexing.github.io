The part that is harder to hire for

Most marketers here can run the channels. Fewer can run the channels and read the customer.

I spent nearly seven years selling luxury to Chinese clients in Singapore. I now market it to Swiss ones.

That is not a language skill, it is a behavioural one. How a client from Shanghai researches a purchase, what reassurance they need before spending, which platform they trust, how they expect to be followed up with afterwards. None of it maps neatly onto European assumptions, and the gap is usually where the sale is lost.

My master's research looked at how Generation Z reads authenticity and trust in digital fashion advertising. The shop floor gave me the instinct; the research gave me a way to test it instead of guessing.

About

My work sits between strategy and execution, and I am happiest when I get to do both: write the plan on Monday and ship the thing on Wednesday.

Retail taught me that brand perception is not built in campaigns, it is built in small interactions: how an item is described, how quickly someone replies, whether the price makes sense. Digital work has not changed that view, it has just given me more places to apply it and better ways to check whether it worked.

Through my academic background I built a foundation in digital strategy, consumer behaviour, content marketing and research. Seven years in luxury and second-hand fashion gave me the other half: how customers actually behave, how a story is told in person, and how brand perception is built one conversation at a time.

How I work

Detail-driven and calm under a long to-do list. I care about clarity and consistency, and about work that holds up when someone looks closely at it.

I do well in small teams where ownership is real and the feedback loop is short, and in roles where marketing, customer experience and brand presentation are not treated as separate departments. I am comfortable running operations and holding the details, and I would rather raise a problem early than present a tidy version of it later.
